Code of Conduct

Member Rules

Short version: attack properly, keep an approved base, don't leak sync times, and don't donate to the war CC. Here's the full list of what's expected from every member.

1War Participation

  • Use both war attacks, every war
  • Attack only your assigned target — no freestyle or trophy-hunting hits
  • Follow the timing called by leadership or reps
  • Tell leadership in advance if you'll be unavailable for a war

2Base & Loot Conduct

  • Keep an FWA-approved base up at all times — no edits during an active war
  • Clear random Clan Castle troops before Battle Day starts
  • Never donate troops, spells, or siege machines to the war Clan Castle
  • Play for loot and account growth, not stars — that's the whole point of FWA

3Sync & Communication

  • Sync times are confidential — never share them outside official channels, on any platform
  • Respond to reps and leadership promptly during sync and match confirmation
  • Report base violations or match disputes through leadership, not directly to opponents
Leaking a sync time is treated as a serious violation, not a minor one — see Consequences below.

4Account Standards

  • Avoid extreme rushing — keep Town Hall, walls, and heroes reasonably in line with each other
  • Maintain hero levels appropriate for your Town Hall
  • Follow both clan leadership and wider FWA guidance (see the Guide for how the system works)

5Consequences

First Offense

Warning from leadership — missed attack, minor base slip, etc.

Repeated

Repeated missed attacks, unapproved base edits, or ignoring rep instructions can lead to a demotion or removal.

Serious

CC donations during war, leaking sync times, or getting the clan flagged/banned result in immediate removal.
